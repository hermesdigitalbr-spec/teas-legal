100% Refund Guarantee If You Don't Pass

In short: the ATI TEAS has no official pass/fail line; it reports an adjusted individual total score from 0% to 100%, and each nursing program sets its own minimum. For this guarantee, "not passing" means an official ATI TEAS adjusted individual total score below 41% (the "Developmental" academic preparedness level on the ATI score report). If you study with the app following the rules below, take the ATI TEAS and score below 41%, we refund your subscription as described here. The guarantee is a commercial promise by Hermes Digital, not a prediction of results: the app is not affiliated with or endorsed by ATI (Assessment Technologies Institute), and no one can guarantee a test result.

1. Who is eligible

  • Be a resident of the United States and at least 18 years old.
  • Have a quarterly subscription. The weekly and monthly plans and the free trial period are not eligible.
  • The subscription must be paid and uninterrupted from sign-up until test day. Any cancellation, billing failure or gap between periods ends eligibility.
  • Have subscribed at least 90 days before your ATI TEAS test date.
  • One Apple ID, on up to two of your own devices. Shared accounts are not eligible.

2. Minimum app use (preparation period)

The preparation period runs from the subscription date to the day before the test and must be at least 30 days long. During it, all of the following are required:

  • Daily time: at least 1 hour 30 minutes of active study per day. Only time with a question session in progress counts — an idle app, locked screen or background time does not.
  • Frequency: every day of the period, without exception. A single day without study ends eligibility.
  • Practice tests: at least 1 full practice test per day in Mock Exam mode, started and finished the same day. Abandoned, restarted or unfinished tests do not count.
  • Volume: at least 100 questions answered per day and 3,000 questions in total during the period.
  • Performance: at least 8 practice tests per calendar month, and an average of 70% or more correct on your last 5 practice tests before the test.
  • Coverage: questions answered in every section of the plan, with no section left at zero, and 100% of your personalized plan completed.
  • Review: clear the missed-questions review queue at least once a week.
  • Streak: end the preparation period with your study streak active since sign-up, with no break.

3. The test

  • Have registered for and taken the full official ATI TEAS (Reading, Math, Science and English and Language Usage), at a testing center or by ATI online proctoring, on your first attempt after your subscription.
  • Have received an official ATI TEAS adjusted individual total score below 41% on the score report issued by ATI.
  • Not covered: absence, dismissal, voided or withheld scores, ATI practice assessments, or admission decisions by any nursing program, including scores above 41% that are below a program's own minimum.

4. How to claim

  • Email support@hermes.com.br with the subject "100% Guarantee" within 7 calendar days after your official scores are released. After that the right expires.
  • Attach: full name; the Apple ID email used for the subscription; your ATI TEAS registration confirmation; your official ATI TEAS score report showing your name and total score; and screenshots of the app's Stats tab.
  • Reply within 7 calendar days to any request for additional information.
  • Authorize us to check your usage records to verify the requirements.

Usage requirements are verified from the records the app keeps on your device. We do not keep a copy of this data on our servers. If the history is lost — by uninstalling the app, switching devices without a backup, or using "Reset Progress" in Settings — compliance cannot be proven and the claim will be declined.

We also decline claims with signs of manipulation: changing the device clock, emulators, tap automation, jailbroken devices, or answer patterns incompatible with a human reading the questions.
